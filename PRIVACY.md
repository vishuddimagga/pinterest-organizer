# Privacy Policy

pinterest-organizer is a personal, single-user tool.

- It accesses only the Pinterest account of its author, with the author's own authorization.
- It reads and modifies only that account's boards and pins.
- Data is stored locally on the author's machine and is not shared, sold, or transferred to any third party.
- No data about other Pinterest users is collected.
- Access tokens are kept locally and never published.
